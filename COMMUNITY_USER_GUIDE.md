# Community User Guide

## Download and open

1. Download `SMW_ROM_Hack_Tracker_Community.xlsm` from the project's GitHub Release.
2. Extract it if it was downloaded inside a ZIP.
3. If Windows blocks macros, right-click the file, choose **Properties**, check **Unblock**, and click **OK**.
4. Open the file in Microsoft Excel desktop.
5. Choose **Enable Content** if Excel shows the security banner.

## Track a hack

1. Open **Tracker**.
2. Select a hack in the **Select ROM Hack** dropdown.
3. The title, creator, exits, difficulty, type, and official links fill automatically.
4. Enter your dates, completion status, rating, playtime, and notes.

## Refresh the official catalog

1. Open **Dashboard**.
2. Click **Refresh Hacks**.
3. Wait for the confirmation message.

Refreshing updates the official catalog only. It does not overwrite your Tracker entries, dates, ratings, playtime, notes, or custom hacks.

## Add a custom hack

1. Fill out the **Add a Custom Hack** form on Dashboard.
2. Click **Add Custom Hack**.
3. The custom hack becomes available in the Tracker dropdown.

Custom hacks are stored inside your copy and remain after catalog refreshes.

## Your copy is private and independent

The workbook does not upload your Tracker progress. Other users cannot edit your entries, and you cannot edit theirs.
